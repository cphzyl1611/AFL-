NOT JSON payloads. Therefore:

1. The harness does NOT invoke JSON body validation
2. The harness does NOT write nv_body_valid_stats.json
3. The harness does NOT invoke the scorer RPC endpoint
4. The scorer process does NOT write scorer_trace.jsonl

at line 1517 correctly recognizes this:
